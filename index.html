About Me

9 Years of Turning
Budgets into Growth

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate who found my calling in digital marketing and built a career around one principle: media spend should move the business, not just the dashboard.

Over 9 years I've worked at global agencies, Indian boutique shops, and high-growth startups, managing budgets from zero to $100M+ and scaling brands across Google, LinkedIn, Meta, and Bing.

From enterprise brands like HP, Oracle, and Pizza Hut at Publicis Media to SaaS scaleups at Triple Dart, to ecommerce and SaaS brands at Yamu. I've operated across every vertical with one obsession: real business outcomes, not vanity metrics.

Beyond campaigns, I work on the full funnel. CRO recommendations, landing page strategy, sales team alignment, ICP definition, and go-to-market planning. The click is just the beginning.
